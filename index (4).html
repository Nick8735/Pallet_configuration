Pallet optimiser

Enter the carton size. The app suggests the best shipper to pack the cartons into, and how the shippers stack on a UK (1200×1000) and a Euro (1000×800) pallet.

The shipper is a simple grid of cartons (no dividers or void fill) with the allowance added on every side. Pallet layouts use a guillotine packing search, which matches the best known pattern for most sizes. Very irregular sizes can occasionally have a pinwheel pattern that squeezes in one more box per layer. Pallet footprints: UK 1200×1000 mm, Euro 1000×800 mm. Cartons stay inside the edge clearance on all four sides.
